# FrankenTerm for iPhone and iPad: Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

FrankenTerm is developed by JeffCo Industries LLC. This policy covers the
native iPhone and iPad app.

## Your connections and local data

You choose the machines to which FrankenTerm connects. SSH authentication,
terminal input, and terminal output travel between your device and those
machines. The operators of those machines may retain their own logs or data.
FrankenTerm does not send that terminal content to a JeffCo-operated service.

Workspaces, host-key trust decisions, preferences, and session history are
stored on your device. The app uses the device Keychain to protect keys for
local encrypted workspace and history storage. Those Keychain entries are
not configured to synchronize through iCloud.

When you export or share data, you choose its destination. Review exported
files and terminal content before sharing them, especially if they contain
hostnames, commands, or other sensitive information. Imported files and the
servers you use remain under your control.

## Collection and tracking

The native app does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or an
analytics service that reports your app activity to JeffCo. It does not
require a separate FrankenTerm account.

Apple processes the App Store purchase under Apple's own privacy policy.
Opening an external link, including the included Mac app download hosted on
GitHub, takes you to a service governed by that service's privacy policy.
Apps or services you choose to use alongside FrankenTerm have their own
data practices.

## Questions

For privacy questions or general support, visit the
[FrankenTerm issue tracker](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/frankenterm/issues).
Do not post passwords, private keys, private terminal output, or other
sensitive information in a public issue.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.
