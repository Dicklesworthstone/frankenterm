# esctest-style cases
esctest/altscreen/1049-switches-to-a-blank-screen Mode 1049 also homes the cursor and resets the pen when it switches (inherited from WezTerm); xterm saves the cursor, switches and clears, leaving the position alone.
esctest/decrqss/sgr DECRQSS answers only DECSCL, DECSTBM and DECSLRM; for SGR (m) it replies 0$r (invalid) where xterm reports the current rendition.
esctest/utf8/lone-continuation-byte A raw 0x80 in the ground state is the C1 control PAD, which executes and prints nothing: the parser keeps its legacy 8-bit C1 contract (ft-fjxga rework). The golden follows Unicode's maximal-subpart practice for a UTF-8 stream, one U+FFFD.
esctest/utf8/encoded-surrogate ED A0 80 shows two U+FFFD: ED and A0 are each a maximal subpart, then the raw 0x80 executes as the C1 control PAD (legacy 8-bit C1 contract, ft-fjxga rework). The golden, following Unicode for a UTF-8 stream, has three.
esctest/utf8/overlong-form E0 80 AF shows two U+FFFD: E0 is a maximal subpart, the raw 0x80 then executes as the C1 control PAD (legacy 8-bit C1 contract, ft-fjxga rework), and AF is a subpart of its own. The golden, following Unicode for a UTF-8 stream, has three.
